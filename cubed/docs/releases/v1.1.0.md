# Picross Cubed v1.1.0

Play feel, and a tightening pass from playing v1.0.

## New

- **Hold to clear a line:** hold on a cube to break (or paint) its whole line, straight
  through the block. A 0 on a full block clears in one go. Hearts mode still stops at the
  first cube of the shape. Shift+X or Shift+Space on a keyboard.
- **Break chips:** a broken cube throws a few chips that fly apart (off with reduced motion).
- **Peel steppers:** − and + beside the peel slider, a layer at a time.

## Fixed

- The peel slider jumped on the first slice (its label wrapped and shifted the page)
- Relaxed counted a block solved even with cubes of the shape broken
- A key pressed after solving solved it again: a story encounter completed twice and
  skipped the next one
- Calling out a forgery right after a mistake dealt the mistake again
- Undo and redo could bring back a fight's wipe or undo a bomb
- A row that's all shape faded its clue as finished before you touched it
- In fights, hints, the line chime, faded clues and screen-reader text used clues the fight
  hides, giving fog and forgeries away
- After a knockout the toolbar came back and the clock restarted
- The charge could go live on a line that could never ground it
- Progress now also saves when the page goes into the background, so a phone closing the
  tab no longer loses your board or fight
- Diorama pieces keep a half-done board; a failed daily clears its save
- Editor: a bad import can't break it any more, undo after an import works, and a rename
  is carried into share links
- Keyboard: call out a forgery, and use the editor's layer grid
- Layout: the fight bar fits a phone, the list title gets its own line, the peel row fits
  360px screens, and the end panel scrolls into view
