# Tier 1: Gadgets

Shown at ~18 px in the gadget tray buttons (`src/ui/screens/run/fight/gadgets.ts`), the
inventory on the path map, and the reward line; at 40 px in the dig reveal. Replaces the
`icon` in `src/puzzles/gadgets.ts`.

Gadgets are objects, not characters: simple, chunky, one accent each. They must read at
18 px, so keep to one big shape and one detail.
