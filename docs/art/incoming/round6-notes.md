## References and continuity

The approved worktree `public/art/cast/rook.png` and `tess.png` are style and scale references for the portraits. Their identities, colors, symbols and backing discs are not part of the new briefs. Reviewer One additionally uses the same-numbered Reviewer Two as a reference for the graphite eye bar only. Pick the same variant number across each set.

Only the three explicitly named phases get new art: Express, Not Stopping; Wrathful Wyrm; Reviewer One. Earlier boss phases and all named regular-enemy phases keep their base sprite, as specified. The 11:59 has no portrait.

## Review priorities

- Portraits: circle fit, head scale, props and the exact expression. No decorative symbols or colored backdrop disc.
- Armored sprites: bold coral gaps or cracks, with coral confined to those weak points.
- The three bosses: large yellow key, with no other yellow. The train has no brass or yellow lamp; the dragon’s coins are paper; Ostrander’s tassel is coral.
- Train clock: no numerals or ticks. Base hands nearly together before twelve; phase hands exactly together pointing up.
- Effects: body-sized fog, soot, smoke and slime. Phase forms retain identity, pose and scale.
- Reviewer One: Professor Ostrander’s body and pose with Reviewer Two’s eye bar, rather than Reviewer Two’s manuscript shield.

Open `round6-review.html` for paired previews on paper, dark surfaces and the dark page color. Portraits appear in 140 px dialogue circles and 52 px sign-off circles, with 40 px square previews. Enemies appear at 38 / 40 px and enlarged, with portrait-to-sprite and base-to-phase comparisons.

## Candidate caveats

The review cards include per-candidate observations. Ostrander and Reviewer One hold their pens upright rather than in the requested horizontal lance pose. Gray shading appears outside several restricted palettes and remains for the normal cleanup pass. Wrathful Wyrm raises the neck and shifts its framing relative to the base; candidate 2 has two long coral gaps rather than three distinct ones. These are candidates for selection, not a claim of exact palette or measured grid compliance.
