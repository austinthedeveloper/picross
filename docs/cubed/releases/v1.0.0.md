# Picross Cubed v1.0.0

The first full release of the 3D sibling of [Picross](https://github.com/austinthedeveloper/picross):
free play, an editor, the daily puzzle, dioramas, badges, and the whole story, on blocks.

Play it at **https://austinthedeveloper.github.io/picross-3d/**. It installs to your home
screen and works offline.

## Free play

- **37 puzzles** from 3×3×3 to 9×8×9: the first 17, plus "Around the house and garden"
  (teapot, doghouse, umbrella, sunflower, cat and more), banded Easy to Expert with filters
- A **guided tutorial** on a real block, and How to play
- A **hint menu**: work out a line, or check for mistakes
- **Views** (front, side, top, reset) and **peels** from either end of each axis
- Finished lines fade their clues; solve times record properly (v0.1.0 recorded 0:00)

## Make and share

- **Editor**: draw a shape layer by layer with a live 3D preview, checked as you draw
  (solvable or not, with the cubes it can't work out outlined), a slider for hidden clues,
  play-testing, share links, and JSON export and import

## Every day

- **Daily puzzle**: one block a day for everyone, turned or mirrored, with a streak
- **Dioramas**: Park and Harbor, big scenes that fill in piece by piece, then turn in 3D
- **Badges**: nine of them in bronze, silver and gold, worked out from your saves

## Story

- The 2D game's **ten chapters**, twenty jobs with Overtime and a boss fight, scenes and
  sign-offs, shared from the 2D game so it stays the one place the story is written
- **57 original 3D puzzles**, one for every encounter
- **Fights** with every 2D power rebuilt for a block: fog, scrambled clues, armor, forged
  clues and call-outs, the charge, ink, the Mimic's wipes and the Weathervane's turns
- **Gadgets** (bomb, patch kit, sponge), power tips, digs and rest stops
- A daily workflow syncs the story from the 2D game and gates anything this game can't
  play yet

## Under the hood

- Installable and offline (a web manifest and a service worker)
- The site serves the latest release at its root and `main` at `/dev/`
- 296 unit tests and 41 phone-sized browser tests, including every chapter from its
  opening to its boss
